How the team works

Build

CAD-first design in Onshape, rapid prototyping, and iterative drivetrain and intake revisions between every competition weekend.

Programming

Java on the REV Control Hub with odometry-assisted autonomous routines, tuned PID control, and driver-practice telemetry.

Outreach

Demos for Iowa City elementary and middle schools, mentoring newer FTC and FLL teams, and STEM nights across Johnson County.

Sponsors

Sponsorship funds parts, registration, and travel — and puts your name in front of our community all season. Sponsor slots are open.
